Privacy Policy for Tashil Data Sharing

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

Tashil Data Sharing ("we", "our", or "the Platform") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our enterprise retail analytics and data ingestion platform handles information when you use our mobile and web applications.

2. Information We Process

Tashil Data Sharing is an enterprise B2B platform. The information processed includes:

  • Account Credentials: Username and encrypted authentication tokens provided by your organization to grant access to authorized dashboards.
  • Business Analytics Data: Retail sales targets, achievement metrics, store branches, inventory levels, and promotional expenses uploaded by authorized users.

3. How Information Is Used

We use the processed data solely to:

  • Provide sales target monitoring, Sell-In/Sell-Out comparisons, and out-of-stock gap analytics.
  • Calculate Trade Marketing and promotional campaign return on investment (ROI).
  • Ensure secure access and authenticate authorized organization personnel.

4. Data Sharing and Protection

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your business or personal information to third parties. All network communications are encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS protocols.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team at: support@sw-point.com.
